# Chip plan — 2026/27

Chips are worth 15–30 points played well and near zero improvised. This file
exists so they are decided in advance and changed deliberately, never in the
last hour before a deadline.

**Each chip is available twice, once per half. First-half chips expire at GW19
and do not roll over.** An unused Bench Boost at GW19 is 15 points binned.

## Status

## Intent

Provisional, written before a ball has been kicked. Revise as fixtures and
blank/double gameweeks are confirmed — usually not announced until the FA Cup
and European rounds settle.

- **Wildcard 1 — target GW8–12.** Early enough to fix draft mistakes, late
  enough to have real 2026/27 data rather than last season's. Do not burn it
  before GW6 on a single injury; that is what transfers are for.
- **Free Hit 1 — hold for a blank gameweek.** Its value is highest when the
  squad is structurally exposed for one week only.
- **Bench Boost 1 — needs a double gameweek** with all 15 playing twice, and a
  bench actually worth boosting. Plan transfers two weeks ahead of it. **Must be
  played by GW19 or it is lost.**
- **Triple Captain 1 — a premium in a double gameweek**, or a standout single
  fixture. Not on a hunch.

## Rule

Before playing any chip, write down what you expect it to gain versus not
playing it. If you cannot put a number on it, do not play it.
